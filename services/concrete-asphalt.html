Frequently asked questions

How soon can you start?

Most small jobs schedule within 1–2 weeks in-season. Larger builds are placed on the production calendar after design approval and deposit.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. For structures or walls that require permitting/engineering, we coordinate with the city and include fees in your proposal.

What’s your warranty?

We include a 1‑year workmanship warranty on installed projects. Manufacturer warranties apply to materials like pavers, lighting, and structures.

Why this matters

Homeowners don’t just want it to look good on day one — they want it to last. Here’s what we do that most people never see:

  • Base compaction to spec; vapor barrier where required
  • 4–5" typical slab thickness; rebar or mesh as specified
  • Expansion/control joints for longevity
  • Broom or stamped finishes; color options available
