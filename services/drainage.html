Drainage & grading solutions

Stop soggy spots and protect foundations—French drains, re‑grading, and surface solutions that move water the right way.

Start My Quote 816-815-1659

Before
After

Great fit when…

  • Water stands after rain or reaches the foundation
  • Downspouts dump near the house or walkways
  • Grass thins in constantly wet areas

What’s included

  • 4″ perforated pipe with fabric sock and washed #57 stone
  • Catch basins and channel drains where needed
  • Downspout extensions / burial to daylight
  • Re‑grading / swales for positive flow

How the project works

  1. On‑site grade check and flow path verification
  2. Proposal with sections for each remedy (good/better/best)
  3. Install with fabric, washed stone, and proper slope
  4. Final grading & seed/straw or sod recommendations

Cost factors

  • Total linear footage & depth
  • Concrete/asphalt cutting & restoration needs
  • Haul‑off and access for equipment

FAQs

Will a French drain fix everything?
It solves sub‑surface water but may be paired with grading or downspout work for best results.
Can you repair lawn after?
Yes—seed/straw or sod options based on season.
